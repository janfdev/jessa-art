07 · The idea
0 × 0

Good soil. Better blooms.

Flourish is a pastel illustrated brand for potting soil, garden tools, patterns, and short video. I built it for a client from a blank page: the wordmark, a palette, a cast of tiny gardeners, packaging, a product line, six seamless patterns, and three looping films.

The whole thing runs on one sentence. Every asset follows the same style description, so a soil bag, a seed packet, and a short video all look like they came from the same hand.

08 · The style
0 × 0

Flat, soft, and a little bit grainy. No outlines. Tiny dot eyes. Flowers bigger than people.

The style, in one paragraph: flat vector-style illustration with soft airy gradient shading and subtle grain, rounded simplified shapes, small faces on characters, a pastel pink, coral, lavender, sky blue, and fresh green palette. Oversized flowers, soap bubbles, translucent rainbow droplets, little gardeners in bucket hats.

11 · Type
0 × 0

Serif for the name, Nunito for everything else.

The wordmark is Fraunces at its largest optical size, weight 600, softness all the way up. Body copy is Nunito. Both are open fonts, shipped in the kit as Flourish Serif.

12 · The bag
0 × 0

The canonical bag.

Arched wordmark, a gardener watering an oversized bloom, a wheelbarrow, seed packets, and a 20L badge. Everything else in the line takes its cues from this one piece.

13 · The cast
0 × 0

The gardeners.

Six little gardeners and six things they use. I kept the people small and the flowers huge, and gave each gardener something to do: watering, planting, hauling pots, pruning from a ladder, sitting with a plant, lugging a sack of soil. They come as transparent PNGs so they can go on anything.

54 · Process
0 × 0

How it was made.

The craft was in the constraints. One style sentence governs every piece, one reference sets the look, and a short list of rules keeps the pieces honest with each other, so the line can keep growing without drifting.

Illustrated pieces stay flat. No outlines, no 3D, no photorealism. Keep the grain and the soft gradients.
Characters are small. Flowers are oversized. Bubbles and droplets are iridescent.
Photoreal only for product and lifestyle. And only if the printed art stays exact.
Clean edges. Transparent characters ship with crisp alpha, so they sit cleanly on any ground.
Loops close. End frame equals start frame, so every animation returns home.
Keep the originals. Every cleaned file ships next to its source, in a folder per asset type.
