ACV Auctions / 2020 – Present · Buffalo, NY

Head of Design

Previously Senior Manager, Product Design; Manager, Product Design; and Senior Product Designer

Led design strategy across three business lines, partnering with 20+ product owners to shape roadmap and priorities while keeping the experience coherent end to end.
Architected and built ACV’s design system from zero: tokens, components, governance. A fragmented multi-team product now reads as though one designer made it, with Figma-to-code parity across 20+ surfaces.
Shipped production features to 100k+ dealers nationwide, including bulk launch flows that compressed launching hundreds of cars into a few clicks.
Led the strategy to evolve the live auction experience, reframing inventory as a two-sided lifecycle and translating it into IA, concepts, and shippable direction.
Unpacked ambiguous, high-stakes problem spaces (assurance, arbitration, personalization) into MVP workstreams and decision-ready artifacts for leadership.
Built the design practice from the ground up: rituals, critique culture, and a career ladder from L1 to Lead. Mentored designers on craft and technical fluency.
Facilitated cross-functional workshops that brought product, engineering, and design to shared decisions faster.
Self-built working prototypes, brand through app, to de-risk concepts and align stakeholders on real artifacts instead of static mocks.
