07 · The idea
0 × 0

One mountain. No colour.

Rime is a high-end mountaineering gear brand I built from a single black and white mountain. A climber hauls himself up a frosted wall, his gear lifts off him into a shop, and the whole thing ends at a campfire by a lake.

Luxury outdoor brands shout in orange and neon. I bet on the opposite: one rounded, frost-dusted peak on a soft grey page, and nothing else until you scroll. If the brand could stay quiet, the gear would feel more expensive.

The name came last. Rime is the frost that forms on rock near a summit. It is exactly what is on the mountain.

09 · The mark
0 × 0

A peak with a diamond in it.

The logo is two clean lines: a soft-topped peak, and a single snow line that turns its summit into a diamond.

That diamond became the whole system. The cursor is a diamond, the scrollbar’s chapter stops are diamonds, the map pins are diamonds. One shape, everywhere, so the brand holds together without needing colour.

The colours are the mountain’s own: a soft grey page, near-black ink, and frosted glass for everything that floats.

11 · The climb
0 × 0

Hand, axe, helmet, then him.

Scroll past the mountain and a new scene slides over it, holding on an empty frosted wall. When it reaches the top, a climber hauls himself up into frame from below: the axe first, then the helmet, then the rest of him. The headline rises in beside him and leans gently toward your cursor.

Then he stands on the summit, seen from behind, and his kit comes off him piece by piece: a ring pulses on it, the product glides out on a soft arc, frosted glass forms around it, and the name and price fade up with a hairline back to where it sat. Helmet, axe, pack, shell, rope, boots.

15 · The shop
0 × 0

A full store, in the same quiet voice.

Behind the story there is a real shop. The wider range sits on glass cards you can filter by climb, wear or camp. Product pages, a floating search, a bag that slides out from the side, and a three-step checkout all speak the same language as the mountain.

I wanted the buying part to feel as calm as the climb. Nothing jumps, nothing shouts, and every page has room to breathe.

20 · Beyond the shop
0 × 0

Somewhere to go, and why it was made.

Expeditions is a dotted world map: solid diamonds for guided climbs, hollow ones for stores, and a dashed arc from the town where you kit up to the mountain.

The Field Journal tells how each piece was made, with prints from the mountain, the original sketch and the story of the problem it solved. Index tabs on the side jump between entries.

The page ends on a quiet film of the same climber cooking outside his tent by a lake, with the mountain behind him, the newsletter on the sky and the wordmark set into the ground. The footer is a campfire.

26 · What I learned
0 × 0

Restraint is the luxury.

Black, white and one grey.Taking colour away made every photo look like it belonged to the same brand.
Let one shape do the work.The diamond in the logo runs through the cursor, the scrollbar, the map and the menu.
Tell the AI less.The smoothest film came from giving it a starting frame and nothing else.
Short pauses beat long ones.A 0.7 second hold on a headline is enough for it to land without anyone feeling stuck.
