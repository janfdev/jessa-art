Our philosophy

Every animal here is a guest, not a show

  • 01Rescue firstMost of our residents came to us injured, stranded or born in care. We never take healthy animals from the wild.
  • 02Room to roamEvery tank is built around how the animal really lives: the depth, the light, the current and places to hide.
  • 03Back to the seaWhenever an animal is strong enough, it goes home. Your ticket helps pay for every release.
