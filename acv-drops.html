07 · The question
0 × 0

From 341,587 listings to one that fits.

ACV Drops is a personalized buy plan for dealers on our wholesale marketplace. It does the navigating so buyers stop guessing and start landing the cars that move. I owned the concept end to end: the seller handoff, the platform’s timing decision, buyer discovery, notifications, and the search results page.

No one product owner owned “the Drop,” so I translated one vision into what each roadmap could carry. A UX researcher with six years of dealer interviews grounded the work. The idea came from buyers before it came from a strategy deck.

30 · Research

Testing the hypothesis

Before pitching a buy plan, I led the design side of a generative study with eight dealers, from one-a-month independents to 500-a-year franchise buyers. How do they actually shop a marketplace this big: what they search, what they save, when they bid, and where the feed lets them down.

39 · Hardest part

The vision touched everything. That was the hardest part.

This cut across rerouted seller flows, buyer notifications, the search results page, and the launch experience, with no single owner for any of it. Alignment meant selling the same story to several partners, then trimming scope so the core could ship. Not everything made the cut.
